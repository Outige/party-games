How the dice work

No buttons mid-fight

Exactly two weapons drop during a fight, roughly 2 and 10 seconds in. Each goes to one random fighter. A new pickup replaces that fighter's old weapon. Its stat boost lasts while held; its special fires once on the next turn.

Both fighters take a turn each round. A d20 attack roll checks for a hit. Defence reduces damage; a block roll can stop even more. There is no fight timer: a knockout decides the winner.
